Fighting for Your Retirement

For decades, SEANC has been the only organization actively standing between state employees and those who would diminish your retirement — and we have a record of winning.

🛡️

Protecting Your Defined Benefit Pension

  • Repeatedly stopped attempts to eliminate the defined benefit pension system and ensured it is fully funded every year by the legislature.
  • In 2016, defeated a bill that would have imposed a minimum retirement age on state employees.
  • In 2025, blocked legislation that would have allowed the State Treasurer to invest 10% of your pension fund in cryptocurrencies.
  • Filed bills requiring the employer's contribution to match at least what employees put in, and has been the key advocate for stable, actuarially recommended funding every year.
⚖️

Holding the System Accountable

  • In 2001, sued over stolen funds from the pension system — and won. The money was returned in full.
  • In 2014, secured the first-ever forensic audit of the retirement system, exposing hundreds of millions of dollars paid to Wall Street money managers in hidden fees.
  • That audit helped elect a State Treasurer in 2016 who ended placement agent fees and the excessive Wall Street payment practice entirely.
  • The only organization consistently demanding transparency in how your retirement funds are managed.
✊

Defending Retirees' Rights

  • When lawmakers tried to impose new taxes on retirees under the Bailey Case, SEANC led the fight to stop them.
  • In 2014, won the return to five-year pension vesting, reversing a change that had extended the requirement to ten years.
  • The reason retirees have a seat at the table during budget negotiations and the reason periodic retirement supplements are secured.
  • No other group actively advocates for state employees in a meaningful way. SEANC is your voice.

Without SEANC, your retirement would look very different.
